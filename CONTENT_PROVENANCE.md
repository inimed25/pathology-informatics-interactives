# Content Provenance and Academic Integrity

This curriculum was developed as an independently authored educational resource in clinical microbiology informatics.

## Educational Content

Educational text, clinical cases, assessment questions, explanations, and original teaching figures in this project are independently authored for this curriculum.

External educational frameworks may be used to identify relevant competencies or curriculum topics. Their text, assessment questions, figures, tables, slides, and other instructional materials are not reproduced or adapted unless reuse is explicitly permitted and appropriately attributed.

## Evidence and References

Factual and technical content is supported by cited authoritative, primary, or peer-reviewed sources whenever appropriate. Sources include professional standards, government and public-health guidance, technical specifications, terminology documentation, and peer-reviewed literature.

Copyrighted standards and educational resources may be cited as supporting references but are not reproduced unless their licenses explicitly permit reuse.

Because laboratory standards, regulatory guidance, terminology, and technical specifications may change, version-sensitive material should be reviewed against the current authoritative source before clinical implementation.

## Curriculum Frameworks

PIER Essentials and educational resources from the Association for Pathology Informatics are used for curriculum alignment, competency mapping, and topic identification. Educational content in this curriculum is independently authored rather than reproduced or adapted from those resources.

## Assessment Questions and Cases

Assessment questions and clinical cases were created specifically for this curriculum. They are not reproduced or intentionally paraphrased from board examinations, commercial question banks, proprietary educational materials, or cited standards.

Cases are educational scenarios and should not be interpreted as patient-specific medical advice or as substitutes for institutional policies, current laboratory standards, or regulatory requirements.

## Figures and Visual Materials

Teaching figures created for this curriculum should be original illustrations based on cited concepts.

Third-party figures, screenshots, tables, or other visual materials should be included only when their licenses explicitly permit the intended reuse. Any such material should retain required attribution and licensing information.

## Use of Sources

Citation of a source indicates that it supports relevant educational concepts; it does not imply endorsement of this curriculum by the source organization or authors.

Where external material is copyrighted or access-restricted, this project links to or cites the source rather than reproducing protected content.
