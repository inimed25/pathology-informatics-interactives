# Lesson–Assessment Alignment

This audit maps the 45 required multiple-choice questions to the concepts explicitly taught in Modules 1.1–1.7. It distinguishes required assessment coverage from concepts that are taught but better suited to optional reinforcement or a supplemental question bank.

## Audit conclusions

- Every required question is supported by its lesson text.
- The required sequence progresses from foundational recognition to applied interpretation, governance, and integrated judgment.
- No question requires a fact or distinction that appears only in its explanation.
- Some secondary lesson objectives are not directly assessed in the shortened core. These are retained as supplemental-assessment opportunities rather than used to expand the required pathway beyond 45 questions.
- The 12-question capstone integrates concepts from all six preceding modules and retains a balanced 3-A/3-B/3-C/3-D answer distribution.

## Supplemental-bank priorities

When the optional bank is implemented, prioritize concepts that are taught but not directly tested in the required flow:

1. Expected phenotypes and provenance of AST rules.
2. Regulatory, accreditation, and professional-guidance distinctions.
3. MALDI-TOF database changes and rapid molecular-panel information models.
4. Hybrid capture versus hybrid assembly.
5. Broader sequencing modalities and selected quality or assembly metrics.
6. LOINC, SNOMED CT, and additional public-health terminology-mapping practice.
