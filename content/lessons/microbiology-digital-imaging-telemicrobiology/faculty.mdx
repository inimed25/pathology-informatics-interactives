# Faculty Guide

## Teaching Focus

This lesson introduces digital imaging and telemicrobiology as tools for remote review, consultation, education, and quality assurance in clinical microbiology.

## Key Teaching Points

- Digital microbiology images can support remote interpretation and consultation.
- Image quality depends on acquisition, focus, resolution, color representation, compression, and display characteristics.
- Successful transmission does not guarantee that an image is diagnostically adequate.
- Telemicrobiology workflows should be validated using representative specimen and image types.
- Remote interpretation should preserve the laboratory and clinical context needed for microbiologic decision-making.
- Laboratories should define escalation pathways when image quality is inadequate or remote interpretation is uncertain.

## Discussion Prompts

- Which microbiology tasks are most suitable for remote image review?
- What image-quality problems could change interpretation?
- What should be included in validation of a telemicrobiology workflow?
- When should a remote reviewer request direct examination of the original specimen or culture?
