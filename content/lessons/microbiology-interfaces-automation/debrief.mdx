# Debrief

Automation can improve microbiology laboratory efficiency by reducing manual data entry and moving results rapidly between instruments and information systems.

However, successful electronic transmission does not guarantee that the meaning of a result has been preserved. Organism codes, antimicrobial results, interpretive categories, and other fields may be transformed or mapped as data move between instruments, middleware, the LIS, and the EHR.

Interface validation should therefore evaluate representative results across the complete reporting pathway rather than simply confirming that a connection is active.

Exception handling is equally important. Failed or unexpected transmissions should enter a visible workflow where they can be identified, investigated, corrected, and reconciled without creating duplicate or missing patient results.
