# Antimicrobial Decision Support and Expert Systems

Clinical microbiology laboratories generate complex antimicrobial susceptibility data that often require interpretation before they become clinically useful.

Expert systems and decision-support rules can help translate susceptibility patterns into meaningful interpretations, identify unusual resistance phenotypes, and support consistent reporting. These tools may exist within instruments, middleware, the LIS, or other clinical systems.

However, decision support is only as reliable as the rules, data, and governance behind it. Rules must be validated, maintained when standards change, and monitored for unintended downstream effects.

In this lesson, you will examine how antimicrobial decision-support systems use laboratory data and where informatics failures can lead to incorrect or misleading interpretations.
