# Integrated Clinical Microbiology Informatics Cases

This capstone applies concepts from the six preceding modules to connected clinical microbiology workflows. The cases ask you to distinguish analytical performance from information-system performance, follow data across system boundaries, and select responses that preserve clinical meaning and patient-result integrity.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this capstone, you should be able to:

1. Integrate microbiology, informatics, quality, and clinical evidence across a result pathway.
2. Identify the primary failure when testing, transmission, mapping, interpretation, or downstream use diverge.
3. Select proportionate validation, verification, monitoring, and change-control responses.
4. Interpret digital and genomic findings within their validated intended use and relevant clinical or epidemiologic context.

The questions are deliberately case based. Use the complete workflow—not a single system or metric—as the unit of reasoning.
