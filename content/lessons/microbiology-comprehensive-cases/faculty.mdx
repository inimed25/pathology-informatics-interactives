# Faculty Guide

Use this capstone after learners complete Modules 1.1–1.6. It is designed as an integrated assessment rather than a source of new terminology.

Ask learners to explain why each incorrect choice is less appropriate, especially when it confuses successful transport with preserved meaning, analytical performance with end-to-end workflow validity, or genomic similarity with proof of direct transmission.

Emphasize proportional reasoning. The strongest response should identify the affected pathway, assess the change or failure, and select verification or validation appropriate to the intended use and clinical risk.
