# Faculty Guide

## Teaching Focus

This lesson introduces antimicrobial decision support and expert systems in clinical microbiology.

## Key Teaching Points

- Microbiology data can be incorporated into rules and decision-support systems.
- Expert systems can assist with interpretation of antimicrobial susceptibility testing.
- Automated interpretation depends on accurate laboratory data and appropriately designed rules.
- Decision-support systems require validation, maintenance, and ongoing oversight.
- Informatics tools should support clinical and laboratory decision-making rather than simply generate additional alerts or information.

## Discussion Prompts

- Where might decision support appear in a routine microbiology workflow?
- What information could an expert system use when interpreting susceptibility results?
- What could happen if an expert-system rule becomes outdated?
- Who should be responsible for maintaining and validating these rules?
