# Digital Imaging and Telemicrobiology

Clinical microbiology has traditionally depended on direct examination of physical specimens, cultures, plates, and microscopic preparations. Digital imaging allows portions of this visual information to be captured, stored, transmitted, and reviewed electronically.

Telemicrobiology extends these capabilities by allowing microbiology images to be interpreted or discussed by individuals who are not physically present in the laboratory. Potential applications include remote consultation, education, quality assurance, and access to specialized expertise.

Successful implementation requires more than obtaining a digital image. Image acquisition, resolution, color representation, display characteristics, network performance, and the clinical context accompanying the image can all influence interpretation.

In this lesson, you will examine how digital imaging can support microbiology workflows and identify the informatics considerations necessary for reliable remote interpretation.
